I am an electrical engineer who keeps industrial plants running and wants to make power grids more resilient. My work sits between the control room and the research paper: optimizing energy use on the factory floor by day, and studying how microgrids can be run cleaner and cheaper.

I graduated from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) in 2023 and have since worked on solar system design, industrial maintenance, and utility operations. I am preparing for graduate study in power systems, smart grids, and cyber-physical systems.

Research

2022 – 2023BUET

B.Sc. thesis: Energy management through optimization of pollutant emission and cost in a microgrid

Supervisor: Dr. Hamidur Rahman, Associate Professor, Department of EEE, BUET

  • Built a MATLAB model of a microgrid with conventional and renewable sources and formulated the energy-management problem as a multi-objective optimization of operating cost and pollutant emission.
  • Applied Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) to find dispatch strategies that balance economic efficiency against environmental impact.
